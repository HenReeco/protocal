ProToCal

A planner for researchers, whether you work at a bench or a laptop.

ProToCal helps you plan research work: keep a backlog of tasks grouped by project, estimate how long things take, place them on a timeline next to your meetings, see at a glance how full each day is, and keep a logbook of what got done. The name says it: Proactive, To-do, Calendar.

How it works

  • Your tasks are stored in Google Tasks, in your own Google account.
  • Your meetings are read from the calendars already on your phone or computer, and never leave it.
  • ProToCal has no server and no accounts of its own. Nobody else, including the developer, can see your data.
