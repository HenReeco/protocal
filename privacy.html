Privacy policy

Daybook. Last updated November 19, 2026.

The short version

Daybook does not collect, store, sell or share your data. It has no server. Everything stays in your own Google account and on your own device.

What Daybook accesses

  • Google Tasks (permission: manage your tasks). Daybook reads and writes the tasks in the one Google Tasks list you choose, so it can show, add, change and complete them. Planning details such as times and estimates are saved in the task's notes.
  • Your email address (permission: see your email address). Shown in the app so you know which account is signed in.
  • Calendars on your device, only if you allow it. Events are read on the device to show them next to your tasks. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Where your data goes

Daybook talks directly to Google's servers from your device. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app. A copy of your tasks is kept on your device so the app works offline, and it is deleted when you sign out or uninstall the app.

Google API Services

Daybook's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can sign out in Daybook's settings, or remove Daybook's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your tasks remain in Google Tasks.
