Daybook

A daily planner that shows how much time you really have.

Daybook helps you plan your days: keep a backlog of tasks without picking a date, estimate how long things take, place them on a timeline next to your meetings, and see at a glance how full each day is.

How it works

  • Your tasks are stored in Google Tasks, in your own Google account.
  • Your meetings are read from the calendars already on your phone, and never leave it.
  • Daybook has no server and no accounts of its own. Nobody else, including the developer, can see your data.
